Sallatunturi / Ski track

Round-the-fell ski track

  • 0.1 km

    from the cabins

  • 7.7 km

    length

The round-the-fell track circles Pieni Pyhätunturi and starts by the ski resort. It is only tens of metres from the cabins, so you can put your skis on in the yard.

  • A loop of about 8–9 km
  • Partly lit
  • Classic and skate
  • Views to the national park and Iso Pyhätunturi

There are barbecue shelters and a hut along the way for breaks. Current track conditions are on the municipality's site.
