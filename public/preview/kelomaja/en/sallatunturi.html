Winter

In winter Salla is best known for skiing, both downhill and cross-country, but there is plenty more to do.

Downhill

  • About 15 slopes for every level
  • Family area for children
  • FIS race slope for experienced skiers
  • Lit slopes for evening skiing
  • Rental, ski school and sport shop

Cross-country

  • 160 km of groomed tracks
  • 45 km lit
  • From short loops to all-day ski tours
  • Tracks kept in shape by Latupooli

More to do

  • Snowshoeing
  • Snowmobile safaris
  • Husky and reindeer rides

Summer and autumn

In summer Salla turns into a paradise for hikers and anyone who loves the outdoors. The midnight sun and the silence make it special, and in autumn the ruska colours the fells.

Hiking

  • Up to 300 km of marked trails
  • From easy day trips to demanding hikes
  • Great views from Ruuhitunturi

Mountain biking

  • Routes for beginners and experts
  • Fell paths, forests and duckboards
  • Ruuhitunturi loop, Sallatunturi paths, Naruska wilderness routes

More to do

  • Paddling on clear lakes
  • Berry and mushroom picking
  • A swimming spot a few hundred metres away

Getting here

  • Train to Kemijärvi (car-carrier train too), then bus to Sallatunturi (about 75 km)
  • Nearest airport about 90 km
