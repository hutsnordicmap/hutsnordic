Questions

Where are the cabins?+

Both are at Sallatunturi: Kelomaja at Revontie 8 B and Kuusisto at Tunturitie 10 A, 98900 Salla. The ski resort, ski tracks and spa are within walking distance.

How many people do they sleep?+

Kelomaja sleeps six in two bedrooms and a loft. Kuusisto sleeps four in a bedroom and a loft, plus two on a sofa bed.

Can I bring a pet?+

Yes. Pets are welcome in both cabins.

How do I book?+

Book on Nettimökki with the button on the cabin's page, or call Arvo on +358 50 539 4738.
