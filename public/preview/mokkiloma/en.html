Questions

How many people do the cottages sleep?+

Elimäki sleeps 12, Meritähti and Merenneito 8, Pöytäkallio and Fransula 6, and Meripoika 5.

What is included in the price?+

A boat and firewood. Bed linen is €12.50 per person and final cleaning is €65–95.

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Weekly stays change over on Saturdays. The cottage is yours from 4 pm on arrival day until noon on departure day. Weekend stays end at 3 pm on Sunday.

Can I rent the smoke sauna on its own?+

Yes. The smoke sauna by the pond takes up to 10 people and costs €160 per time. You can add the island grill shelter and crayfishing.

Where are the cottages?+

In Yläne, Pöytyä, around Lake Elijärvi, about 12 km from Yläne village and 65 km from Turku, Finland. Fransula is on the Säkylä side, on Koivulantie.

Do the cottages suit company events?+

Yes. They suit company meetings and recreation days, and the smoke sauna, grill shelter and a crayfish party can be booked for the same day.

How do I book?+

Call +358 400 534 973 or email mikameri@netti.fi with the cottage, your dates and the number of guests.
