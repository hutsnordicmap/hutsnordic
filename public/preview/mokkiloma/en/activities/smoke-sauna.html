Smoke sauna and crayfish pond

  • Up to 10 people
  • €160 per time
  • Island grill shelter and arched bridge
  • Crayfishing in our own pond

The farm has its own pond with a smoke sauna on its shore. In the pond is an island with a cosy grill shelter, reached by a “romantic” arched bridge. We farm crayfish in the pond, so crayfishing is always possible. These can be rented separately, so you can spend an unforgettable day or evening with your staff or friends, for example at a crayfish party. From the smoke sauna and the yard sauna you can swim with the crayfish, or in the river that never freezes.
