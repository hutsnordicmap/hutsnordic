Elimäki

A round-log villa high on a hill, with its own shore sauna

Best for: big families, groups of friends and company days

This round-log villa is the “crown” of our cottages! The house stands high on a hill, with a separate sauna down on the shore. It sleeps 12. The cosy living room has a “starry sky” ceiling and a large slate fireplace. Two covered terraces. The roomy changing room of the shore sauna has an open fireplace.

What the cottage has

  • Sleeps 12
  • Separate shore sauna, open fireplace in the changing room
  • “Starry sky” ceiling and a slate fireplace
  • Two covered terraces
  • Private hot tub for 11 (extra charge)
  • Boat and firewood included
