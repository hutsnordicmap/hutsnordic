Event room · Sammatti

A small party and meeting room in an old mill

The event room in the mill's lower wing seats 25. Everyone fits at one long table, or the table becomes a buffet and the room gets a garden feel with space to dance.

  • Seats 25
  • €50 per hour
  • Bookable 15 May–15 Aug
  • Coffee sets for 32

Catering your way

Bring your own tableware, food and drinks, use an outside caterer, or order from the mill café. The bakes on the café's main spread are gluten-free.

Coffee sets for 32 people can be hired for €0.50 per set (washing-up fee). A set is a side plate, saucer, coffee cup and spoon.

The room has an American-style fridge with a freezer. There is no kitchen or cooking, and no projector.

Two ways to set the room

All 25 guests can sit together at one long table. Or the long table turns into a buffet at the back of the room, and light terrace chairs and glass tables bring in a garden feel. This layout also leaves room for dancing.

Meetings and family occasions

The room suits meetings, birthdays and other small family occasions. It has hosted, for example, a joint party for two 80th birthdays. The room can be booked from 15 May to 15 August by arrangement. Ask for free dates by text message or WhatsApp, +358 44 016 5047. The event room is step-free.

Questions

What does the room cost?+

€50 per hour in the summer season.

How many people fit?+

25, all at one long table. With a buffet layout there is also room to dance.

Is there a kitchen?+

No. There is a fridge with a freezer and coffee sets for 32, but no kitchen and no projector.

Can I bring my own food?+

Yes. Bring your own, use a caterer or order bakes from the mill café.
