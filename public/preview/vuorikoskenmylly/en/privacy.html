What we collect

When you call, email or message us (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram), we get your name, contact details and your message. For bookings and orders also the dates, number of people and your wishes.

Why

To answer your questions and handle stays, event room bookings and cake orders. The legal basis is the contract or preparing it, and Finnish accounting law.

How long

Messages and booking details are deleted when no longer needed. Accounting records are kept for the time the law requires.

We never sell or pass on your details. Messages travel through the email and messaging services you use.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details using the contact details above. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
