Vuorikosken Mylly/Café

The mill café · Sammatti

A gluten-free café in an old water mill

Fresh coffee, tea and gluten-free sweet and savoury bakes made on the farm, in a traditional mill setting.

  • Every bake gluten-free
  • Baked on the farm
  • Own mango-citrus iced tea and iced latte
  • Pets welcome

Baked on the farm, gluten-free

Every sweet and savoury bake in the mill café is made on the farm, and all of them are gluten-free. If you are coeliac, you do not need to ask what you can have.

On hot summer days, Vuorikoski's own mango-citrus iced tea and iced latte cool you down.

Bring the dog

Pets are welcome in the café too. There is fresh drinking water on the terrace, and pressed turkey treats are handed out at the counter.

Breakfast for overnight guests

Guests staying in the mill cabins can order a plated breakfast, served in the café at an agreed time and tailored to each guest's diet.
