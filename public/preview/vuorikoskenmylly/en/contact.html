Hours and contact

The café and vintage shop are open from Easter to the end of August on holiday weekends and every Thu–Sun from 17 June to 8 August, 11–17, then on All Saints' and Father's Day weekends and as a Christmas shop on Advent weekends, 11–16. The yard has parking for 20 cars.

Accessibility

The yard has parking for 20 cars. The parking area, yard paths, outdoor seating and the event room are step-free. A ramp leads into the mill, with a low threshold at the door. The gallery, toilet, sauna and cabins are up or down steps, so they do not suit visitors with limited mobility.
