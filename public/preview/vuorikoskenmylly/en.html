The mill café

A gluten-free spread and fresh cinnamon buns

The café serves fresh coffee, tea and gluten-free, lactose-free sweet and savoury bakes made on the farm, with a vegan option every day. A small side table holds the day's fresh wheat cinnamon buns. On hot summer days, Vuorikoski's own mango-citrus iced tea and iced latte cool you down.

1893

History

The mill ground grain, sawed timber and made electricity

Built in 1893 in the Sammatti countryside, the mill ground grain for the cooperative's members, sawed timber and even produced electricity for the village. The farm later passed into private hands and took the name Vuorikoski after its owner at the time.

Today the farm is home to an entrepreneur couple working in visual fields. Restored to its original look, the mill is open in the summer season and on Advent weekends before Christmas. In 2023 the mill turned 130, and summer 2026 marked five years since the sign went up by the road and today's Vuorikoski began.

The makers

The story of the makers behind the mill

What happens when two hands-on country boys are kept too long in a cramped city flat? It can spark an unstoppable urge to renovate and dig, which flares up like a bonfire once the right place comes along. That is how they arrived in Myllykylä, Sammatti, at an overgrown plot with an old mill at the far end that had ended up as a junk store. Yes, we'll take it!

A couple of years later the thicket had become a garden and the buildings were restored to the look they deserve. Why are we sitting here on our own? What if we opened the place to the villagers? Let's put the handiwork piling up in the sheds on sale in the yard barn and open the doors of the museum mill. The clock struck eleven, the first customers came, then the next and the next, and the flow never stopped.

Now, five years later, Vuorikosken Mylly has become a shared living room for locals and summer cottagers, welcoming thousands of visitors every year. Everyone is welcome here!

Questions

Is there gluten-free food in the café?+

Yes. The sweet and savoury bakes made on the farm are gluten-free and lactose-free, and there is at least one vegan option every day. A separate small table also has the day's fresh wheat cinnamon buns.

Can I bring my dog to the café?+

Yes. Pets are welcome in the café. There is fresh drinking water on the terrace and pressed turkey treats at the counter.

When is Vuorikosken Mylly open?+

From Easter to the end of August on holiday weekends 11–17 and every Thu–Sun from 17 June to 8 August, then on All Saints' and Father's Day weekends and as a Christmas shop on Advent weekends, 11–16. All the days are on the Hours & contact page.

Where is the mill?+

At Tavolantie 18, 09220 Sammatti. Sammatti is part of the town of Lohja in West Uusimaa, Finland.

Can I get there by bus?+

Yes. A bus runs from Lohja to Sammatti, to the Myllykylä stop, which is a 200 metre walk from the mill.

Can I stay overnight?+

Yes. There are two small off-grid summer cabins for 1–2 people by the mill pond, and you can add the shore sauna and breakfast. Ask for free dates by phone or WhatsApp.
