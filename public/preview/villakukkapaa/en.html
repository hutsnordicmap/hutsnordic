The spacious and comfortable Villa Kukkapää is in Sulkava, on Lake Saimaa, 330 km from Helsinki. This historic villa, once the head office of the Sulkava sawmill, has been renovated with respect for the old and equipped for today.

It suits smaller and bigger groups, and visitors to the Savonlinna Opera Festival are frequent guests thanks to the convenient location, under 40 km from Savonlinna.

The villa

250 m², tiled stove and fireplace, sleeps 10

Lakeside sauna

AitoKiuas stove, dining for 10, beds for 4

A private sand beach

Over 200 m of private sand beach where the water grows gently deeper, so it suits small children too. A pier, rowing boat, canoe and a grass field for volleyball and badminton.

1922–1951

The sawmill manager's house

Gunnar and Edith Blomqvist lived here from 1922 to 1951. The mill office was in the house, and a couple was married in its dining room in 1938.

Questions

How many people does Villa Kukkapää sleep?+

The villa sleeps 10, and the lakeside sauna house has beds for 4 more.

Where is Villa Kukkapää?+

Vekarantie 462, 58700 Sulkava, Finland, on Lake Saimaa in the Vilkaharju landscape. Savonlinna is under 40 km away and Helsinki 330 km.

Can I rent the villa in winter?+

Yes. The villa and the sauna house are both winterised, electrically heated and have air-source heat pumps. Firewood for the tiled stove and fireplaces is included.

Can I bring a pet?+

Small pets are welcome. The 20,000 m² grounds have a wooden fence.

How do I book?+

Bookings go through Lomarengas, where you also find the booking terms. The key handover and a short tour are arranged with Pentti.

What do linen and cleaning cost?+

A linen set (bed linen and towels) is €30 per person, and final cleaning is €200.
