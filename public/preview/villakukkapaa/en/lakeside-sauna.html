The lakeside sauna house

Villa Kukkapää has a big sauna chalet right on Lake Saimaa. The sauna has two separate stoves: the traditional AitoKiuas heated by wood, and an electric stove for quick and easy use. Besides beds for four, the sauna house has its own kitchenette and lounge.

From the terrace, a wooden pier reaches over the sand beach into the lake.

What's there

  • Wood-fired AitoKiuas and an electric stove
  • Lounge and dining for 10
  • Beds for 4
  • Kitchenette
  • Open fireplace and a terrace on the lake
  • Washroom with 2 showers and a toilet
  • Drying cabinet
  • Used in winter too, air-source heat pump

Heating the sauna and fireplaces

In this video, Pentti shows how to heat the AitoKiuas, the tiled stove and the open fireplace. The AitoKiuas is a one-time-heated stove: heat it for a couple of hours, then enjoy the sauna for a long time without adding wood.
