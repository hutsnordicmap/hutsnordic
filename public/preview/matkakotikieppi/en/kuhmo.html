Kuhmo

Kuhmo Chamber Music, the Kalevala and Kainuu nature

Matkakoti Kieppi is in the centre of Kuhmo. People come for the music in summer, the ski trails in winter and the Kainuu wilderness all year.

  • Chamber Music in July
  • Juminkeko 200 m
  • Petola about 3 km
  • Ski trails about 3 km

Kuhmo Chamber Music

Kuhmo Chamber Music is held every July. In 2026 the festival ran from 12 to 25 July. Ask for rooms early for the festival weeks. Breakfast is served all through July.

Juminkeko and the Kalevala

Juminkeko, the Kalevala information centre, is only 200 metres away. The town's restaurants, Atalante library, the swimming hall and Ruukinranta beach are all under a kilometre away.

Nature and outdoor sports

Petola, Kuhmo's nature centre, presents Finland's large carnivores and has a nature trail, about three kilometres away. The lit loops at Kalevala sports centre link to a ski trail network of about 80 kilometres.

Questions

Can I walk to the concerts?+

We are in the centre of Kuhmo, and the town's services are within walking distance. Juminkeko is 200 metres away.

Should I book early for Kuhmo Chamber Music?+

Yes. Kuhmo is busy during the festival, so ask for a room well in advance.

What is there to do in Kuhmo in winter?+

The lit ski loops of Kalevala sports centre are about 3 km away, and the swimming hall is under a kilometre away.
