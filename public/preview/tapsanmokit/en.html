Questions

Where are the places?+

The villa is on Lake Iso-Uurainen at Täkylahdentie 42 B, Uurainen. The cottage is on a pond at Sikomäentie 365, Kuikka. The apartment is in Tikkakoski at Tikkakoskentie 527, 18 km from Jyväskylä city centre and 4 km from the airport.

How many people do they sleep?+

The apartment sleeps five. The cottage sleeps 4+2, with more beds in the guest house. The villa has three bedrooms and an alcove. For a bigger group, ask for a quote.

Can I rent the hot tub on its own?+

Yes. It is €80 a day on weekdays and €140 a day at weekends. Pick it up, or we deliver the trailer.

How do I book?+

Call +358 400 244 406 or send a quote request from the place's page. The availability calendar shows the booked days.
