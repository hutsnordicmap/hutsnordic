Villa Suvikallio

A five-star villa at the tip of a narrow cape

  • 4 + 2 guests
  • 2 bedrooms
  • 90 m²
  • 2000

Villa Suvikallio was built in 2000. It stands in a quiet spot at the tip of a narrow cape, and the lake shows from every window. It holds a five-star MALO rating.

What the villa has

  • Five-star MALO rating
  • Year-round, 180 mm log
  • Lake view from every window
  • Separate wood-heated shore sauna
  • Hot tub (extra charge)
  • Large grill hut, beach volleyball court and rowing boat

Rooms

2 bedrooms, living room, kitchen, sauna and toilet on two floors, about 90 m². 4 + 2 guests.

Equipment

  • Wood-burning fireplace
  • Air heat pump and electric heating
  • Electric sauna
  • Electric cooker and microwave
  • Fridge-freezer
  • Dishwasher
  • Washing machine
  • Coffee maker and toaster
  • TV, DVD player and CD radio

The hot tub costs extra. Ask for the price when you book.
