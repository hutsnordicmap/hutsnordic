Villa Onnela

A new log villa with a child-friendly sandy beach

  • 6 + 2 guests
  • 3 bedrooms
  • 145 m²
  • 2015

Villa Onnela was completed at the end of 2015. Everything is on one level, and the sandy beach deepens gently, so it is ideal for families with children. The villa is allergy-free, and pets cannot be brought along.

What the villa has

  • Year-round, 200 mm log
  • Separate wood-heated shore sauna
  • Sandy, gently deepening beach
  • Sleeping cabin, barbecue shelter and carport
  • Rowing boat
  • Allergy-free, no pets

Rooms

3 bedrooms, living room, kitchen, utility room, sauna and toilet, about 145 m². 6 + 2 guests.

Equipment

  • Wood-burning fireplace
  • Geothermal heating
  • Electric sauna
  • Electric cooker and microwave
  • Fridge and freezer
  • Dishwasher
  • Washing machine and tumble dryer
  • Coffee maker and toaster
  • TV, DVD player and CD radio
