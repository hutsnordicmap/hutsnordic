A holiday on Lake Puula

Puula is one of the large lakes of South Savo, known for its clean water. The villas are at Läsäkoski in Kangasniemi, along Nurmelantie, in their own peace on the shore.

Summer is for rowing, fishing and swimming. In autumn the nearby forests are full of berries and mushrooms, and in winter the log walls and fireplace keep you warm.

Questions

How many people do the villas sleep?+

Villa Onnela sleeps 6 + 2 and Villa Suvikallio sleeps 4 + 2.

Are the villas open in winter?+

Yes. Both are winterised log villas and are rented all year round.

Is there a lakeside sauna?+

Yes. Each villa has an electric sauna inside and a separate wood-heated sauna on the shore.

Can I bring a pet?+

Villa Onnela is allergy-free and pets cannot be brought along. For Villa Suvikallio, please ask when you book.

Where are the villas?+

On the shore of Lake Puula at Läsäkoski, in Kangasniemi, South Savo, Finland. The address is Nurmelantie 209, 51430 Läsäkoski.

How do I book?+

Call us or email varaukset@onnelanmokit.com. You can also book both villas on Airbnb.
