Owner Antti Jakola (with Juha), antti.jakola@hotmail.com, 044 016 5047. The business is a café, vintage and
interior shop, summer gallery, event room for 25, two small B&B cabins and a shore sauna, at an 1893 water mill,
Tavolantie 18. Business ID 1770144-7. Old site: Webnode, vuorikoskenmylly.com (domain bought through Webnode,
expires 29.6.2027). Preview: https://hutsnordic.fi/preview/vuorikoskenmylly. Source: `sites/vuorikoskenmylly/`.

## What made it work

- Questions on each page that shrink as he answers. He kept sending.
- Every email was turned into live pages within the hour, with a short reply naming what changed and the link.
- We chose for him: what to leave out, where photos go, how to phrase things. He was told why once and stayed
  in charge.
- Jeff barely had to follow along. He forwarded emails and approved drafts.
